Enable Speculative Loading

speculative.loading.rules
WHAT IT DOES

Tells the browser to quietly load a page the visitor is likely to open next, so it appears instantly.

WHAT IT COSTS YOU
Prefetch downloads the page in the background, which uses bandwidth the visitor may never benefit from. Prerender goes further and renders it, costing more CPU and memory. Conservative eagerness only acts on hover or touch, which keeps the waste low.
